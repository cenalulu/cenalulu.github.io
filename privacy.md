*Last updated: October 28, 2026*

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit
[cenalulu's Tech Blog](https://cenalulu.github.io/) ("the site"), how it is used,
and the choices you have. By using the site you agree to the practices described here.

## Information I collect

I do not ask you to create an account, and I do not collect personal information
such as your name or email address through the site itself. If you leave a comment or
email me, I only see what you choose to send.

Like most websites, the site and the third-party services it uses may automatically
receive technical information from your browser, such as your IP address, browser type,
device type, operating system, referring page, pages visited and the time of your visit.

## Cookies and third-party services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses the following third-party services, which may
set cookies or use similar technologies in your browser:

- **Google AdSense** (advertising). Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to
  serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this site or other websites. Google's use of
  advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit
  to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
- **Google Analytics** (traffic statistics). Used to understand how many people visit the
  site and which pages are read. Learn more in
  [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- **Baidu Tongji** (traffic statistics). A web analytics service provided by Baidu.
- **Disqus** (comments). If you view or post comments, Disqus may collect data according to
  its [privacy policy](https://help.disqus.com/en/articles/1717103-disqus-privacy-policy).
- **Social sharing buttons** (such as Twitter/X, Facebook and JiaThis). When you click or
  load them, those services may collect information under their own privacy policies.

I do not control the cookies set by these third parties. Please review their own
privacy policies for details.

## Your choices

- **Personalized advertising.** You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting
  [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/). You can also opt out of
  some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at
  [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/) or, in the EU,
  [www.youronlinechoices.eu](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/).
- **Google Analytics.** You can install the
  [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
- **Browser settings.** You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some
  parts of the site may not work as expected if you do.

## Children's privacy

The site is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal
information from children.
